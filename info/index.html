What Happens Next

From signing to streaming - here's how we work together.

1

Sign & Kickoff

We start with a collaborative strategy session. Get to know each other, align on goals, and map out the vision for your releases.

2

Build Together

We develop the release strategy together, planning content, building a narrative, and aligning the creative direction for marketing.

3

Pre-Release

We inject the sound into the ecosystem before release. Test content, build momentum, seed the right communities, and get everything primed.

4

Launch & React

Release day hits and we go into our main campaign. We monitor performance in real-time, double down on what's working, and optimise in real time.

5

Ongoing Support

We're in it for the long run. We provide continuous support, strategic advice, and are able to react to ensure your success as you and your records evolve.
